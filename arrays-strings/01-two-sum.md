# Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

Link: https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach

I used a nested loop to check every possible pair of numbers in the array. If the sum of two numbers is equal to the target, their indices are printed.

## Complexity

Time: O(n²)
Space: O(1)

## Notes

Tested the program with two test cases. The first is a normal case and the second checks duplicate values.
